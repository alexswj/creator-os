Creator OS

A private campaign tracker for Nicolette Wee

Creator OS is a private tool that Nicolette Wee and her manager use to run her own brand partnerships: quotes, invoices, deliverables and the performance of her posts. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

With her permission, it reads statistics about her own Instagram and TikTok posts so that campaign reports for the brands she works with are accurate.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Who this covers

Creator OS is a private tool used only by Nicolette Wee and her manager. The only social media accounts it connects to are her own, with her permission.

What it reads

  • TikTok: her basic profile (display name and avatar) and, for her own public videos, the number of views, likes, comments and shares, the posting date and the link.
  • Instagram: for her own posts, the number of views, reach, saves, shares and interactions, the posting date and the link.

It does not read messages, followers' personal information, or anything about other accounts.

How it is used

The figures are used only to track her campaigns and to report a campaign's performance to the brand that commissioned it. They are never sold, and never shared with anyone else.

Where it is kept

All data is stored on her own computer. The sign-in that lets Creator OS read her accounts is kept in the computer's secure keychain. Nothing is stored on a server run by Creator OS.

Disconnecting and deleting data

Access can be withdrawn at any time: in Creator OS (Setup → Connections → Disconnect), in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services) or in Instagram (Settings → Apps and websites). To have stored statistics deleted, email nicolette.wee@gmail.com and they will be removed within 30 days.
